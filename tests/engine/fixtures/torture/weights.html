Overflows (clipped in HTML)

This card has more copy than room. The browser clips it with overflow:hidden, so the design looks tidy, but the text frame in PowerPoint cannot clip — the last lines spill below the card and over whatever sits underneath it on the slide. Nothing in the file says the copy was cut, so the reader of the export would see words the designer never saw. Shorten the copy, or give the card the room it needs.

Copy that reaches the very bottom edge of the card with its last line, which is common in dense panels.
